1. Self-Drive Vehicle & Motorcycle Rentals

Q: What documents do I need to rent a motorcycle or self-drive car?

Indian citizens must present an original valid Driving License (LMV for cars, 2-Wheeler for bikes held for at least 12 months) and an original Aadhaar Card / Passport for identity verification. Foreign nationals require an International Driving Permit (IDP) alongside their national license.

Q: How does the security deposit work?

A refundable security deposit (varies by vehicle model) is collected at vehicle handover via UPI, Cash, or Card. Upon vehicle return and physical inspection, the deposit is refunded within 24 hours after checking for traffic e-challans.

Q: Are helmets provided with bike rentals?

Yes, one ISI-certified helmet for the rider is included free with every motorcycle rental. An additional helmet for the pillion and protective riding gear (jackets, gloves, knee guards) can be rented on request.

2. Chauffeur-Driven Mountain SUVs

Q: Are your chauffeurs experienced in mountain driving?

Absolutely. All Tripdhara drivers are mountain veterans with a minimum of 5+ years of driving experience across steep Himalayan gradients, monsoon fog, and winter snow routes in Uttarakhand and Himachal.

Q: Are toll taxes, state taxes, and fuel included in chauffeur fares?

Yes. All chauffeur-driven package quotes include vehicle fuel, driver allowances, toll taxes, and commercial state entry taxes (AITP permits). There are no hidden fees.

🏡

3. Homestays & Resort Reservations

Q: How are homestays and resorts verified?

Every property listed on Tripdhara is pre-inspected by our local ground team for cleanliness, mountain views, power backup, hot water availability, and hygiene standards.

Q: What is Form-C registration for foreign nationals?

Under Indian Immigration laws, all hotels and homestays must submit Form-C details for foreign guests. Our team assists guests in submitting passport/visa copies upon check-in to ensure 100% legal compliance.

⚡

4. Weather Advisories & Cancellations

Q: What happens if a landslide or heavy snow closes my trip route?

If official authorities (IMD / BRO / PWD) close a route, our ground team immediately assists with safe alternative rerouting or issues a 100% 12-Month Travel Credit Voucher with zero cancellation penalty.

🪪

5. Inner Line Permits (ILP) & Border Passes

Q: Do I need special permits for Chopta, Niti Valley, or Spiti?

Restricted border sectors near the Indo-Tibet border (e.g. Niti Valley, Nelang Valley, Harsil, or Spiti interior passes) require Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) Inner Line Permits. Tripdhara coordinates permit processing when identity documents are submitted 72 hours in advance.
